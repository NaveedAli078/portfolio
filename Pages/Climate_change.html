Overview

Monitoring a Changing Planet

Climate change is reshaping precipitation patterns, temperature regimes, and hydrological cycles across South Asia. Pakistan — already vulnerable to extreme weather — is experiencing intensified monsoons, glacial lake outburst floods (GLOFs), and prolonged droughts within the same decade.

Remote sensing and cloud computing now enable continuous, large-scale monitoring of these changes. Through time-series analysis of multi-decadal satellite archives, we can quantify trends, detect anomalies, and support evidence-based climate adaptation policy.

Current Research

Rawal Dam Study

"Analyzing Hydrological and Operational Changes in Rawal Dam Due to Climate and Land Use Variations" — ongoing research paper integrating multi-source remote sensing, climate data, and GIS spatial analysis.

This study examines how 30+ years of climate variability and upstream LULC change have altered inflow patterns, sedimentation, and operational reliability of Rawal Dam — a critical water supply for the Rawalpindi–Islamabad metropolitan area.
