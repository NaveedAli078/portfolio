Overview

Intelligence Meets Geography

GeoAI is the convergence of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and geospatial science. It transforms how we extract information from satellite imagery, automate spatial analysis workflows, and predict geographic phenomena at scales that were previously impossible with manual methods.

From classifying land cover across entire countries in hours using Google Earth Engine, to detecting flooded buildings from SAR imagery with deep learning — GeoAI is reshaping the boundaries of what geospatial professionals can achieve.

Areas of Application

🏷️

LULC Classification

Supervised and semi-supervised classification of land use and land cover using Random Forest, SVM, and neural networks trained on Sentinel-2 and Landsat imagery.

🌊

Flood Susceptibility Mapping

Ensemble ML models (RF, XGBoost, LightGBM) for flood susceptibility and hazard assessment using DEM, hydrology, and climate conditioning variables.

🔍

Object Detection (Remote Sensing)

CNN-based detection of buildings, vehicles, and infrastructure in high-resolution satellite imagery using YOLO and U-Net architectures.

📡

Change Detection

Deep learning-based bitemporal change detection for identifying urban expansion, deforestation, and post-disaster damage from multi-temporal satellite pairs.

🌡️

Spatial Prediction Modeling

Regression and classification models for predicting spatial phenomena — groundwater potential, erosion risk, and crop yield — from multi-source geospatial features.

☁️

Cloud-Scale ML with GEE

Running machine learning classifiers at planetary scale inside Google Earth Engine — no local compute required, full satellite archive access.

Workflow

GeoAI Pipeline

"A rigorous GeoAI workflow combines multi-source feature engineering, careful training data curation, spatial cross-validation, and uncertainty quantification — not just model accuracy on a random split."

My ML workflows follow these key stages: (1) feature engineering from spectral bands, indices, and terrain derivatives, (2) training sample collection via stratified random sampling, (3) model training with hyperparameter tuning, (4) spatial cross-validation to avoid data leakage, (5) probability map generation and threshold optimization, (6) accuracy assessment with independent validation data.
