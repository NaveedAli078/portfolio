Overview

Protecting Critical Ecosystems

Wetlands, forests, and water bodies are the foundations of healthy ecosystems — regulating water cycles, sequestering carbon, supporting biodiversity, and providing livelihoods for millions of people. Yet they are disappearing faster than at any point in human history.

Remote sensing provides the only cost-effective means to monitor natural resources continuously at large spatial scales. Spectral indices derived from multispectral satellite imagery can detect vegetation health, water extent, and land cover change with remarkable accuracy — enabling early warning systems and evidence-based conservation planning.

Spectral Indices

Remote Sensing Toolkit

"Spectral indices — NDVI, NDWI, MNDWI, EVI — provide simple yet powerful proxies for vegetation, water, and land surface properties that can be computed from freely available satellite data at global scale."

My workflow applies these indices within Google Earth Engine, enabling pixel-level change detection across years of cloud-filtered satellite composites without local computing bottlenecks.
