01 — About Me

GIS Specialist | &
Geospatial Analyst

I hold a BS in Geo-informatics (GIS & Remote Sensing) from Arid Agriculture University Rawalpindi, with a focus on remote sensing, GIS, geospatial data science, and GeoAI for multidisciplinary applications. I have over 2+ years of GIS industry experience across real-world projects spanning land records, fiber network planning, drone surveys, and population analytics.

My ultimate goal is to develop new techniques to improve the accuracy and effectiveness of climate change monitoring, flood hazard mapping, landslide risk assessment, and water resource management. I am also passionate about building open-source AI models that solve real problems for the GIS community.

I am also a freelance geospatial consultant on Fiverr, providing geospatial analysis, web mapping, data visualization, and Google Earth Engine services.
